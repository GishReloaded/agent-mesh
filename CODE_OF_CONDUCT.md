# Community conduct

AgentMesh welcomes contributors with different backgrounds and levels of experience.
Be direct, civil and specific. Critique ideas and code, give others room to learn,
and keep discussions relevant to the project.

Harassment, threats, discriminatory abuse, unwanted personal contact and disclosure
of someone else's private information are not acceptable. Do not publish credentials,
private chat logs or identifying information in examples or bug reports.

Maintainers may edit or remove harmful content, close disruptive discussions and
restrict participation where needed. When possible, explain the reason and allow
the person to correct their behaviour.

Report problematic public content using GitHub's reporting controls. For a project
security vulnerability, follow [SECURITY.md](SECURITY.md) rather than opening a public issue.

These expectations apply to project issues, pull requests and discussions. They do
not replace GitHub's own policies.
